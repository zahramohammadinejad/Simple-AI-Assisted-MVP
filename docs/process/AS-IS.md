# AS-IS Process

## Current-state scenario

Employee requests are assumed to arrive through informal channels such as email or chat.

```text
Employee
   ↓
Email / Chat
   ↓
Manager
   ↓
Manual routing
   ↓
Department
   ↓
Policy check
   ↓
Back-and-forth for missing information
   ↓
Approval
   ↓
Manual execution
   ↓
Employee notification
```

## Problems

1. Requests are unstructured.
2. Routing is manual.
3. Required information is frequently missing.
4. Policy checks are manual.
5. Approval paths can be inconsistent.
6. Employees have limited status visibility.
7. Follow-up is manual.
8. Audit history is fragmented.
9. Routine requests consume management time.
10. Sensitive requests may require stronger controls than informal channels provide.

## Business impact

- longer cycle time
- unnecessary manual work
- avoidable routing errors
- inconsistent process execution
- weak traceability
- poor employee experience

This AS-IS model is a portfolio reference process and does not claim to represent a specific real organization.
